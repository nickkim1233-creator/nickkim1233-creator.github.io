Part 01
Where to buy & trade crypto
Three main places. Each with real trade-offs.
🏦
Type 01
Centralized Exchange

A company holds your crypto. Sign up with email and password — they handle security for you.

CoinbaseKrakenBinanceGemini
Easiest start — sign up and buy
Account recovery if locked out
Bank transfers and cards
You don't truly own the coins
Can freeze accounts; regulated
⚙️
Type 02
Decentralized Exchange

Trade directly from your own wallet. No company, no signup. Smart contracts run everything.

UniswapJupiterRaydium
You hold your coins at all times
Access to thousands of tokens
No support — mistakes are permanent
Requires a self-custody wallet first
👻
Type 03
Self-Custody Wallet

An app giving total ownership. You get a seed phrase — the master key. Lose it and your funds are gone forever.

PhantomMetaMaskLedger
Complete ownership — only you
Connects to any DEX
Lost seed phrase = permanent loss
No customer support
→
Where to start: Buy on a centralized exchange like Coinbase. Once comfortable, move some to Phantom to explore DEXs and full ownership.

Part 02
Moving crypto without losing it
This is where most mistakes happen. Read the first section before anything else.
🧠
The one concept you must understand first
Read before any transfer
▾

The same coin can travel on different networks — and each network requires a different address format.

⚠
Sending on the wrong network is the #1 way people permanently lose funds. Your coin reaches a valid address — but on the wrong network, unreachable forever.

Scenario 2 — BTC via Solana: Coinbase → Phantom
Tricky · Wrapped tokens
▾
⚠
"BTC via Solana" is not real Bitcoin. You're sending cbBTC — a Solana token. You need a Solana address, not a Bitcoin address.
1
Open Phantom → switch to Solana
44-character address, no "bc1" prefix.
2
On Coinbase, select cbBTC (not regular BTC)
3
Network = Solana → paste Solana address → send
!
Never paste a Bitcoin address when the network is Solana. Funds are unrecoverable.
💎
Scenario 3 — ETH: Coinbase → MetaMask
Ethereum and its side-networks
▾
→
MetaMask's 0x address is the same on all EVM chains. But sending via Polygon means it arrives on Polygon — separate balance from mainnet.
1
Copy your MetaMask address (0x…)
2
Choose your network on Coinbase carefully
Mainnet = slow, pricey. Base/Arbitrum = fast, cheap.
3
In MetaMask, switch to the same network
✓
Wrong EVM network? Recoverable — just add that network to MetaMask and switch.
🪙
